> Outline for the Risk Management Plan. Risks themselves are **Risk (5x5)** work items.

## 1. Approach

How risks are identified, analyzed, planned, tracked, controlled, and communicated; who owns the process;
how often the risk board meets.

## 3. Handling approaches

Mitigate, watch, research, accept, elevate, close: when each applies and who may approve it. Acceptance
of a risk is a decision; record who accepted it.

## 4. Reporting

Which risks go to which board, and what is shown at life-cycle reviews and key decision points.
