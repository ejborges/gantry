## 2. Approach

### 2.1 Verification methods

| Method | When it is used |
|---|---|
| Test | Performance can be measured under controlled conditions |
| Analysis | Compliance is shown by models, simulation or calculation |
| Inspection | Compliance is shown by examining the item or its records |
| Demonstration | Function is shown by operation, without detailed measurement |

### 2.2 Levels of assembly

Component, subsystem, system, mission.

### 2.3 Model philosophy

Engineering, qualification, protoflight and flight units, and what is tested on each.
